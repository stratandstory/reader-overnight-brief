# Every source and its terms

[Back to the start](../README.md) · [Sources and their terms](guide/05-sources-and-terms.md)

Generated from `config/sources.toml`. **Terms** is what the publisher's terms of use say about automated reading and AI use, as read on October 1, 2026: `official` (a government, central bank or regulator publication), `no-clause` (no clause against automated reading or AI use), `restricts` (the terms restrict automated access, AI use, or both), `not-read` (not reviewed). **Read under** lists the choices (`./brief choose-sources`) that include the source. The summaries paraphrase the terms; the terms themselves are on each publisher's site, and they change. This is not legal advice.
